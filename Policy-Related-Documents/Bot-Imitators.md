# Bot Imitators

## I. Introduction

This file was created in reference to **§II. Discord Bots** of our [Authenticity document](/Transparency/Authenticity.md "Authenticity – Compendium Standard Public Disclosures") and flows from it to provide information to explain the risks of interacting with a Discord bot that imitates Compendium, how to identify it, and how to report false bots.

### 1. Reasoning

Compendium is a bot designed to collect information for legitimate purposes, namely to gather information from users and server staff, to aggregate it, and to deliver it to any interested parties on the server. We are a service provider for you as the user or server staff. Compendium is governed by a [Terms of Service](Terms-of-Service.md "Terms of Service – Compendium Standard Public Disclosures") and a [Privacy Policy](Privacy-Policy.md "Privacy Policy – Compendium Standard Public Disclosures"), which clearly explains the relationship between you, the user, and us, the service provider. These agreements are standard practice among legitimate service providers on Discord.

The following should be assumed of any bot which falsely assumes Compendium's identity, but is not Compendium, or the same pattern is exhibited in regard to any of Compendium's other bots listed in **§II. Discord Bots** of our [Authenticity document](/Transparency/Authenticity.md "Authenticity – Compendium Standard Public Disclosures"):

- The bot's false identity was assumed for the purposes of deception, with the intent to conceal another, less trustworthy purpose.
- The particular assumption of Compendium as its identity is to mimic Compendium's legitimate functions, such as collection of information from users.
- The element of deception used suggests that its purposes are illegitimate.
- Absent a terms of service or privacy policy presented by the false bot, the developer of this false bot also stores and uses your data without binding declaration of purpose, and handles your data without promise or guarantee of its security, usage, and either denies of obfuscates means to exercise your rights over it as governed by applicable laws.

## II. What can bots do?

Discord bots are applications which interact with Discord, and which require authorization from users and/or server administrators in order to perform functions necessary to the bot's services. By creating an application on Discord, Discord application developers are bound by a separate [Discord Developer Terms of Service](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8562894815383-Discord-Developer-Terms-of-Service), and [Discord Developer Policy](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/8563934450327-Discord-Developer-Policy). These policies govern what applications can and cannot do in relation to Discord and its users.

#### 1. All of the following can occur with or without notifying you that the information has been gathered.

***Once authorization is given, a bot can, among others:***

- Read information from commands, modals, drop down menus, buttons, and other interaction methods, given to it within a server.
- Read information from messages sent in a server if it has access to read messages in the channel.
- Read information from messages sent in a DM or Group DM with it, regardless of authorization.
- Read user information such as username, user activity, or in authorization circumstances allow, the email connected to your Discord account.
- Store any information gathered on private servers, with or without without lawful recourse to request or demand its disclosure, correction, or removal to you as the interested party.

### II. How can I tell that a bot is fake?

If you do not see the bot in the below list, you can inspect the bot user:

1. Enable “**Developer Mode**” in __User Settings__ *(Click the* ⚙️ *icon)* → **<>** __Developer__ *(Should be near the bottom)*.
2. When enabled, locate the bot account, and:

   1. If you are using a mobile or tablet device, long-press on the bot's profile row in the members tray on the right. This opens the account's server profile. Select “**Copy User Info**”, then “**Copy User ID**”.
   2. If you are using a desktop or laptop computer, right-click on the bot's profile row in the members tray on the right, and select “**Copy User ID**”.
3. Paste the ID somewhere. If the ID is not represented within **§II. Discord Bots** of our [Authenticity document](/Transparency/Authenticity.md "Authenticity – Compendium Standard Public Disclosures"), then the bot is fake.

If you determine a bot to be fake, please see the instructions on how to report it to us below.

### III. What can I do when I find a false bot?

Unfortunately, Discord gives little recourse to us as developers to resolve this issue. You as the user(s) have the following possibilities for recourse:

1. Limit your exposure to any false bot. This means not remaining in servers where it is present, not inviting it to any server, blocking the bot, and certainly not providing any information while in a space shared by it.
2. Report the false bot by the methods prescribed in **§I.(3)(A): How to report fraudulent activity** of our [Authenticity document](/Transparency/Authenticity.md "Authenticity – Compendium Standard Public Disclosures"). Please include what evidence you are able to include so we can determine if the bot is fake, and where it occurs.
3. [Reporting this application to Discord](https://discord.com/safety/reporting-applications-to-discord).

### IV. How can I request updates to any information written here?

First of all, thank you very much for your care for this information's accuracy!

Similar to reporting new false bots, you can provide updates to findings by the methods prescribed in **§I.(3)(A): How to report fraudulent activity** of our [Authenticity document](/Transparency/Authenticity.md "Authenticity – Compendium Standard Public Disclosures"). Please include what evidence you are able to include so we can determine if the bot is fake, and where it occurs.

### V. Known Fake Bots

Compendium will not publish a list of bots known to us to be fake. As we have published the identity of the real bots in the original [Authenticity document](/Transparency/Authenticity.md "Authenticity – Compendium Standard Public Disclosures") and the means to determine any fakes, all bots which assume the identity of a real Compendium service, but which fail to meet the authenticity criteria provided are therefore sufficiently determined to be false.
